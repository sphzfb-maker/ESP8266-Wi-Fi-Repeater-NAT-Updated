This firmware has just been customized for your build (MAC clone, client
list, EN language, reconnect stability), so no prebuilt .bin is shipped
here to avoid confusion with an out-of-date binary.

Build it yourself in one step:
    ./flash.sh /dev/ttyUSB0      (Linux/macOS)
    flash.bat COM3               (Windows)

These scripts now run `pio run` to compile, then flash the result
straight from .pio/build/esp12e/firmware.bin — that's where the .bin
will appear if you ever want to grab it directly instead of flashing.
